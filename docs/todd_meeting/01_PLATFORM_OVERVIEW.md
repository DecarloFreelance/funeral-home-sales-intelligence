# Funeral Industry Outreach & Sales Intelligence

## Built by Alex De Carlo / Digital Pathway

### The original problem

Todd originally wanted practical help building funeral-home contact lists,
marketing prepared material, researching funeral associations, and following up
consistently. The recurring difficulty is not simply finding names: it is knowing
which organizations fit an offer, finding usable public contact information,
understanding why each prospect was selected, and keeping outreach organized.

### What has been built

Alex has built a working research and outreach-support platform for the funeral
profession. It can:

- discover Canadian funeral-home organizations from controlled public sources;
- consolidate duplicate listings instead of counting them as separate prospects;
- research public websites, business contact details, named people and roles;
- identify evidenced services and aspects of an organization's digital presence;
- prioritize suitable prospects instead of working a list randomly;
- prepare a relevant commercial angle tied to the public evidence for that
  organization;
- support human-reviewed outreach preparation and follow-up;
- record research, review, contact, reply, meeting and proposal stages; and
- reconcile manually sent messages so a prospect is not approached twice by
  mistake.

The current Canadian dataset contains **211 consolidated organizations across
all 10 provinces**. Public phone information is available for **207** and public
email information for **64**. Those figures describe current research coverage,
not consent, deliverability, or guaranteed suitability for a campaign.

### Why this matters to Todd

The same underlying intelligence asset can support several focused campaigns:

- **Grief-information seminars:** select funeral homes by geography and relevant
  public evidence, then validate the right contact and prepare a tailored first
  approach.
- **Convention presentations:** build and review funeral-association target lists
  across Canada and, after a separate US research phase, the United States.
- **Celebrant training:** identify associations and funeral businesses that fit
  a defined training offer, while keeping campaign history separate and clear.
- **Follow-up campaigns:** see who has been researched, reviewed, contacted or
  replied, and what action is due next.

This avoids rebuilding disconnected spreadsheets for every offer. One maintained
research base can be segmented differently for seminars, presentations and Life
Celebrants International training.

### A controlled business process

The platform supports research and preparation; it does not replace business
judgment. Alex still decides whether a prospect is appropriate, reviews the
evidence and message, controls sending, interprets responses, and handles
commercial conversations. Public contact information is checked before use, but
it is not treated as consent or as proof that an individual mailbox is active.
The objective is a more focused, explainable and disciplined outreach process—not
automatic selling or guaranteed results.
