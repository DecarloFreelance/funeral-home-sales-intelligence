# Identified Gaps

## Contact Verification Gap

Current contact selection relies primarily on extracted website data.

Limitations:

- Email ownership is not verified.
- Decision-maker identity is inferred.
- Phone ownership is not confirmed.

Future improvements:

- Email verification
- Business registry matching
- Decision-maker enrichment


---

## Personalization Depth

Current personalization is rule-based.

Limitations:

- Limited company-specific observations.
- No executive biography enrichment.
- No recent business activity signals.

Future improvements:

- Leadership discovery
- Social/business profile enrichment
- Recent company event analysis


---

## Message Optimization

Current messaging uses predefined templates.

Limitations:

- No A/B testing.
- No response tracking.
- No learning feedback loop.

Future improvements:

- Campaign performance tracking
- Reply classification
- Message optimization


---

## CRM Intelligence

Current CRM output provides readiness information.

Limitations:

- No external CRM synchronization.
- No activity tracking.
- No pipeline history.

Future improvements:

- CRM API integrations
- Follow-up automation
- Sales activity history

# Audit Findings

## Existing Capabilities

Verified:

- Outreach package generation
- Campaign classification
- Personalized hooks
- Sales message alignment
- Executive summaries
- Follow-up sequencing
- CRM status generation


## Architecture Assessment

Current outreach intelligence is operational but heavily rule-based.

The foundation supports automation, but future phases should introduce:

- Verified contact enrichment
- Feedback-driven optimization
- External intelligence sources
- Sales outcome tracking
