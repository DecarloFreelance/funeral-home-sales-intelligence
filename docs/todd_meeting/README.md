# Todd Meeting Pack

This directory is a business-development pack for Alex's Zoom or FaceTime
conversation with Todd.

| File | Purpose | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `01_PLATFORM_OVERVIEW.md` | One-page explanation of the business problem, working platform and relevance to Todd | May be sent to Todd |
| `02_30_DAY_PILOT_PROPOSAL.md` | Recommended CAD $900 pilot, smaller option, ongoing rate and friendly ownership terms | May be sent to Todd after scope is confirmed |
| `03_ZOOM_CALL_CHEAT_SHEET.md` | Opening, questions, pricing answers, objections and closes | **Keep open beside the Zoom window; internal only** |
| `04_EVIDENCE_AND_EXAMPLES.md` | Verified metrics, pilot status and defensible examples | Internal support; do not send unchanged |
| `05_FOLLOWUP_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md` | Short post-call recap with placeholders for actual decisions | Customize and send only after the meeting |

Recommended call use: keep the cheat sheet open, use the platform overview only
if Todd wants a concise explanation, and introduce the pilot after discovering
which offer matters most. Do not send the evidence document or quote its internal
pilot history without deciding that the detail is useful and appropriate.
