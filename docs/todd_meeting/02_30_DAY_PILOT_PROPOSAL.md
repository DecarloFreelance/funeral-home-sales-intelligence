# 30-Day Funeral Home Outreach Pilot

## Objective

Validate one of Todd's commercial offers against a carefully selected segment of
funeral homes. The recommended first offer is Todd's **grief-information
seminars**, beginning with a deliberately bounded Canadian segment.

## Scope

- Agree on the seminar offer, audience, geography and definition of a useful
  response.
- Research and qualify approximately **25–50 organizations**, with the final
  number set after the segment and available contact coverage are reviewed.
- Validate the public business contact and the evidence supporting each choice.
- Align a concise outreach message and follow-up sequence with Todd.
- Prepare, review and track the campaign; sending remains human-controlled.
- Report results and recommend whether to refine, stop or expand the test.

The pilot is intentionally small enough to review well. It is a validation
engagement, not a promise of response, conversion, revenue or legal compliance.

## Deliverables

- Agreed target and qualification brief
- Prioritized prospect list with public contact details and selection rationale
- Reviewed message and follow-up sequence using Todd's approved material
- Campaign tracker showing current stage and next action
- End-of-pilot results summary and scale recommendation

## Schedule

### Week 1 — Define and prepare

- Confirm the target, offer, geography and success definition.
- Select prospects and validate contact/evidence quality.
- Align messaging with Todd's seminar materials and voice.

### Week 2 — Begin outreach

- Complete final human review before each initial message goes out.
- Start the bounded outreach sequence and record activity.

### Week 3 — Follow up and qualify

- Follow up where appropriate.
- Classify responses, questions, interest and opt-outs.
- Identify conversations Todd should handle personally.

### Week 4 — Complete and assess

- Make a final follow-up where appropriate.
- Analyze results and outstanding actions.
- Recommend changes and whether a larger campaign is justified.

## Tracked outcomes

- Organizations researched and qualified
- Public contacts checked and messages sent
- Responses and positive responses
- Meetings or conversations
- Opt-outs and prospects not pursued
- Follow-ups due

Results will be reported as observed pilot outcomes, not predictive conversion
rates.
