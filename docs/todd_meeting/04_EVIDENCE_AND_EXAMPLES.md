## Selected real-world examples

### Beaverlodge Funeral Service

The system detected a live first-party “Pre-Arrange Online” pathway after a
real-world review exposed a gap in the wording detector. The detector was
improved narrowly, the current evidence stayed tied to Beaverlodge, and the
outreach angle remained reviewable and tracked rather than making an unsupported
claim.

**Business capability demonstrated:** the research can improve when current
public evidence reveals a gap, while keeping the resulting message tied to what
was actually observed.

### Foster and McGarvey

A malformed association website value could have created a false organization
identity. The import process was hardened to reject that malformed identity
while preserving the valid Foster and McGarvey organization and contact data.

**Business capability demonstrated:** source lists are not blindly trusted;
identity and contact quality are checked before a prospect enters the usable
research base.

### Gregory's Funeral Home

A manually sent message had to be reconciled with the local campaign history.
The workflow was strengthened so prior contact is considered across prospect
selection and draft preparation, preventing the organization from being treated
as a fresh first contact.

**Business capability demonstrated:** contact history matters, including manual
sends outside the platform, so duplicate outreach can be prevented.

### Cornerstone Funeral Home

The workflow retains the reviewed, evidence-specific prepared message rather
than showing an older generic preview after preparation.

**Business capability demonstrated:** the operator sees the current approved
working material and its actual lifecycle stage, reducing confusion during
follow-up.

### Fernhill Cemetery Company

Fernhill has an evidence-bound review angle, while implementation access remains
classified as unknown until a human confirms who controls the website.

**Business capability demonstrated:** a commercially useful observation can be
prepared without pretending that Alex already has the access or authority to
implement a change.

### Mission View and Foothills Memorial Chapel

Current first-party evidence supports specific pathway observations, but likely
website-provider involvement still requires human confirmation. Foothills also
demonstrated why a bounded scan must not be turned into an absolute claim of
absence when a live pathway is visible.

**Business capability demonstrated:** the system can prepare implementation
questions and cautious commercial angles while preserving human judgment and
avoiding overstatement.

## What is not automated / still human-controlled

- Choosing Todd's priority offer, audience and acceptable campaign boundaries
- Deciding whether a prospect is appropriate for a specific offer
- Interpreting ambiguous ownership, branch and association relationships
- Final review and approval of every outreach message
- External or manual sending where applicable
- Handling replies and deciding whether a conversation is commercially useful
- Confirming customer website/provider access before implementation
- Quoting, negotiation, contracting and commercial commitments
- Legal/compliance judgment for a particular message or recipient

The platform supports these decisions with organized evidence and history; it
does not make them on Todd's or Alex's behalf.
